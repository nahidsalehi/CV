About

Research grounded in real systems and real data.

I am an AI/NLP engineer and researcher with a dual M.Sc. and research and development experience across multilingual retrieval, information extraction, machine learning, and healthcare AI. I currently work as a Research & Development Specialist at the University of Isfahan.

My recent work has moved increasingly toward large language models, information retrieval, structured and unstructured data, and evaluation. In a recent industry role in healthcare AI, I contributed to team-based work on LLM-assisted information extraction from clinical documentation and gained hands-on experience with clinical-text reliability, validation against clinician-curated ground truth, extraction-quality analysis, and safety-oriented failure handling.

I am especially interested in PhD research where LLMs and machine learning are used carefully: systems that are useful in practice, grounded in evidence, evaluated rigorously, and designed for high-impact domains such as health.

What I want to work on next

LLMs & Clinical NLP

Reliable extraction and reasoning over structured and unstructured medical text, with attention to temporality, assertions, and model failure modes.

Information Retrieval & RAG

Trustworthy Applied AI

Human-gated evaluation, traceability, grounded outputs, reproducibility, and practical methods for understanding when AI systems should or should not be trusted.

Data Analysis

Applied analysis of heterogeneous real-world data, including extraction-quality evaluation, anomaly detection, and evidence-based comparison of model behavior.
