About

I am an AI/NLP engineer and researcher currently working as a Research & Development Specialist at the Information Technology, Security and Intelligence Development Center, University of Isfahan. My recent work focuses on multilingual information retrieval, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), LLM-based information extraction, and applied data analysis. I also have recent industry experience working as part of a healthcare AI team on LLM-based workflows for clinical text.

I hold a dual M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and Game Engineering from the University of Isfahan and Keimyung University in South Korea. My current research interests include large language models, clinical NLP, information retrieval and RAG, trustworthy AI, and applied machine learning for real-world data.
